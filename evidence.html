Four-language speech pipeline

Bulbul-generated speech → Saaras transcription → English translation → extraction. Six author-written cases per language. Synthetic quiet audio from the same provider is easier than real people speaking in a clinic. CER is a normalized character error rate; lower is better. Lexical critical checks cover selected numbers and denials, not full semantic correctness. Speech-only success excludes the extraction step. Failed extractions were rechecked using saved translations; earlier successful rows were retained. Latency includes the original attempt plus recheck where applicable, not a fresh pipeline benchmark.
